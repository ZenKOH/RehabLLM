CLINICAL GUIDELINE01 / 04

Robot-assisted arm training after stroke

NICE NG236 recommends against offering robot-assisted arm training as part of an upper-limb rehabilitation programme after stroke (recommendation 1.13.18, 2023).

Interpret carefullyThis is a specific guideline recommendation—not a verdict on every rehabilitation robot or research intervention.
NICE · NG236 · 2023Primary source ↗
FOUNDATIONAL CONCEPT02 / 04

Rehabilitation is larger than robotics

The World Health Organization frames rehabilitation around optimising functioning and reducing disability in the context of a person's health condition and environment.

Interpret carefullyMore devices, sessions or repetitions do not automatically establish meaningful gains in daily life or participation.
WHO · Fact sheetPrimary source ↗
CLINICAL GUIDELINE03 / 04

Electromechanical gait training

NICE NG236 recommends offering electromechanical gait training after stroke only in the context of a research study (recommendation 1.13.24, from 2013).

Interpret carefullyResearch-only guidance indicates an evidence or implementation question; it does not establish that a device is universally ineffective.
NICE · NG236 · 2013Primary source ↗
DATA PROVENANCE04 / 04

Open access does not mean unrestricted reuse

PubMed Central distinguishes between material that is readable online and content licensed for reuse. Applicable permissions depend on each article's terms.

Interpret carefullyEvery training or retrieval pipeline needs article-level rights checks, attribution and compliant collection methods.
NIH / NLM · PMCPrimary source ↗

02 — INSIDE THE PROJECT

An actual model. And an honest boundary.

RehabLLM is a compact decoder-only Transformer implemented from scratch in PyTorch. It exists to make training, evaluation and data decisions inspectable.

03 — RESEARCH METHOD

Transparent by design.

Good research records what is known, what remains uncertain, and where a model fails.

01

Collect with rights

Document article-level permissions, provenance and inclusion criteria. Remove duplicates and publishing artefacts.

Run reproducible experiments
